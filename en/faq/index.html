FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services does Bogotá SEO offer?

SEO, local SEO and Google Business Profile, AI search visibility, web design and online stores, Google, Facebook and Instagram ads, social media management, WhatsApp chatbots, AI agents and process automation. You can choose just one or combine them based on what your business needs.

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on the service, your competition and your starting point. Optimizing your Google Business Profile or launching ads can show up within weeks; organic SEO usually takes several months. A website is typically delivered in a few weeks. We give you clear estimated timelines in the quote.

How much does it cost to work with you?

There's no single price because every business needs something different. Cost depends on how many services you take, the size of the website, the competition in your industry and how much content is needed. Quotes are free with no obligation: message us on WhatsApp and we'll explain options based on your goal and budget.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

Terms depend on the service, and we explain them in writing before we start, in plain language. Some work, like SEO, needs months to pay off, so we recommend thinking medium term. You always know what you're paying for and what you get.

Can you serve me in Spanish?

Yes. Stephany, who grew up in Bogotá, leads client relationships in Spanish, and the content we write for your business is in Colombian Spanish. If your business also serves English-speaking customers, we can work in both languages.

Where are you located?

We specialize in Bogotá and the Sabana and work with businesses across Colombia and in other countries. The Eye To Ad Media team behind the technology is headquartered in Denver, Colorado, and works with businesses all over the world.

What's the fastest way to reach you?

WhatsApp. Tap the green button on any page and tell us what you sell and where you serve. If you prefer, leave your details on the contact form and Stephany or someone on the team will write to you.

Is it safe to use AI agents with my customers?

Yes, when it's done right. Agents work with rules you set, answer only what we authorize and hand sensitive cases to a person. We review the quality of their answers and you can see the conversations. AI helps; people stay in charge.

What do you do with personal data?

We handle data in line with Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012. We only use it for authorized purposes, protect it with security measures, and you can access it, correct it or ask for it to be deleted. It's all explained in our personal data treatment policy.

Do you guarantee first place on Google or more sales?

No. No honest company can guarantee Google rankings or a number of sales, because they depend on many factors nobody fully controls. What we do guarantee is serious work, honesty and the best tools, with a monthly report showing what was done and what moved.

Is it true you sometimes give away marketing?

We sometimes give bonus marketing at no charge to loyal clients and to people who refer good clients, at times worth thousands of dollars. It's a thank-you, not a promise or a sign-up incentive: don't sign up expecting free marketing.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp or request a free SEO audit. We review how your business shows up on Google, Maps and AI tools, and whether your website converts. Then we suggest where to start and send you a no-obligation quote.
