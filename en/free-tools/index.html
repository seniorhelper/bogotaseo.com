    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Do the tools save my data?

    No. They all run inside your browser. What you type isn't sent to any server or to our team. If you'd like help with the results, message us on WhatsApp.

    How accurate is the lost-customers calculator?

    It's an estimate based on the numbers you enter. It helps you size how much money you might be missing by replying late; it isn't a prediction. Use your real numbers to make it useful.

    Can I use the generated messages in WhatsApp Business?

    Yes. Copy and paste them into the free tools in the WhatsApp Business app: greeting message, away message and quick replies. Replace the bracketed details with your address and links.

    How do I create a WhatsApp link for my business?

    Enter your country code, your mobile number and an optional opening message. The tool builds a wa.me link you can put on your website, on Instagram or in a QR code so people can message you with one tap.
