The problem

A great product nobody can find is a very well-kept secret

Bogotá is full of excellent businesses: the bakery with the best pan de bono in the neighborhood, the auto shop that actually delivers on time, the consultant who truly knows their stuff. The problem is almost never quality. It's that when someone searches on Google or asks their phone, someone else shows up.

According to the Bogotá Chamber of Commerce (CCB), there are 496,686 active companies in the Bogotá region, and 81,372 new ones were created between January and November 2025. Standing out no longer depends only on being good. It depends on being found, on a website that shows what makes you different, and on making it effortless to message you.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SEO and why does it matter for my business in Bogotá?

SEO, or search engine optimization, is the work that gets your business to show up on Google, Google Maps and AI answers when someone searches for what you sell. For a small business in Bogotá it means being found by people who already want to buy, without paying for every click.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on your competition and starting point. Some changes, like optimizing your Google Business Profile, show up within weeks; strong organic rankings usually take several months. We never promise dates or the top spot, but we show you the progress every month.

Do you also build websites and online stores?

Yes. We design phone-first websites with a WhatsApp button, fast load times and SEO built in from the start. We also build online stores and custom projects based on what your business needs.

What is an AI agent and what can it do for my company?

An AI agent is software that does a complete task for you: it answers WhatsApp messages, organizes files, builds reports, requests reviews or follows up on quotes. It always works within rules you set and hands important cases to a person.

Do you only work in Bogotá?

We specialize in Bogotá and the surrounding Sabana, but we work with businesses across Colombia and in other countries. The team is headquartered in Denver, Colorado, and Stephany helps you in Spanish or English on WhatsApp.

How much does it cost to work with Bogotá SEO?

Every business needs something different, so we quote custom with no obligation. Message us on WhatsApp or request your free SEO audit and we'll walk you through options for your goal and budget.
