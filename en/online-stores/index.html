Quick answer

The short answer

What does an online store in Colombia need to sell?

An online store that sells in Colombia needs three things: a catalog that's easy to browse on a phone, payment methods people already use, and shipping or delivery explained with no fine print. We design professional online stores on those foundations, plus WhatsApp orders and SEO so your products can be found on Google.

Many small businesses in Bogotá sell through Instagram and direct messages, one at a time, and it works until there aren't enough hours in the day. Answering the price a hundred times, sending bank details, confirming addresses, losing orders between chats... A well-built online store organizes all of that and gives you time for what you do best: your product.

What's included

Online store design built for Colombian shoppers

Clear catalog

Categories that make sense, useful filters, fast-loading photos and product pages with everything customers ask: sizes, dimensions, materials, delivery times.

Local payments

We connect your store to a payment gateway. Depending on the payment gateway you choose, you can accept PSE (Colombia's bank transfer system), debit and credit cards and wallets such as Nequi or Daviplata.

Shipping and delivery

Clear rules by area: local delivery in Bogotá and the Sabana, national shipping, in-store pickup. Customers know how much and when before paying.

WhatsApp orders

A button on each product opens WhatsApp with the product name already typed. Ideal for customers who want to ask before buying.

Product SEO

A professional online store is also built for Google

A store nobody can find is a shop window on a street with no traffic. That's why we build every store on SEO foundations from the start.

  • Product names written the way people search, not just the way the supplier names them.
  • Original descriptions for each product, not copied from the manufacturer.
  • Compressed photos with descriptive file names and alt text.
  • Product, price and availability structured data so Google understands them.
  • Categories that match real searches, for example "women's running shoes" instead of "Line 3".
  • Clear shipping, exchange and return pages, which also build trust.

With good foundations, your products have the opportunity to appear on Google. We don't promise rankings or sales: every product competes differently and results vary. What we do is build it right and measure.

A possible case

What it could look like for a real small business

Example

Imagine a clothing brand made in Bogotá that sells on Instagram from a workshop in Kennedy. When it moves to an online store, it uploads its catalog with sizes and real photos, connects a payment gateway, sets up local delivery in Bogotá and national shipping, and adds a WhatsApp button on every item. Instagram still shows what's new, but now each post leads to a product page where the customer can buy without waiting for a reply.

The same setup works for a pharmacy (droguería) delivering in Suba, a bakery selling custom cakes or an auto parts shop. The store adapts to the business, not the other way around.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to have an online store in Colombia?

You need your product catalog with photos and prices, a way to receive payments through a payment gateway, a shipping or delivery policy, and data and return policies. We help you organize all of it, set up the store and get it ready to receive orders through the website and through WhatsApp.

Can my online store accept PSE, Nequi or Daviplata?

Payment methods depend on the payment gateway you choose. Many gateways operating in Colombia offer PSE and cards, and some also offer wallets like Nequi or Daviplata. We help you compare options for your type of business and connect the one you pick to your store.

Can I receive orders on WhatsApp from my online store?

Yes. Many customers in Colombia prefer to confirm on WhatsApp before paying. We can add a button on each product that opens a chat with the product name already typed, so you handle the order quickly and clearly. You can also have a chatbot answer the most common questions.

How do I get my products to show up on Google?

Each product needs a clear name, its own description, optimized photos and structured data Google understands. Categories should also match what people search for. We build the store on those SEO foundations, though nobody can guarantee rankings; results vary with each product's competition.

Is it better to sell only on Instagram or have an online store?

Instagram is excellent for showing products, but the account isn't fully yours and selling through one-by-one messages takes a lot of time. An online store gives you your own catalog, organized payments and the chance to appear on Google. Ideally the two work together.

How much does an online store cost?

It depends on the number of products, whether photos need to be taken or edited, the payment gateway, shipping rules and the integrations you need. That's why we prefer to understand your business before giving a figure. The quote is free and in writing.
