Google Business Profile: we set it up, verify it and keep it active

Short answer

What is a Google Business Profile and what do we do with it?

Google Business Profile is the free listing that shows your business on Google Maps and in search: address, hours, photos, reviews and buttons to call, message or get directions. It used to be called Google My Business, and in Colombia many people still search for it as "Google Mi Negocio." We create or claim it, help you verify it, optimize it and keep it active every week.

Google changed the name in late 2021, but the Keyword Planner data we use still shows thousands of searches a month for "Google Mi Negocio" in Colombia. That's normal: the old name stuck. What matters is that, whatever it's called, for many businesses in Bogotá this listing is the first impression customers get, often even before the website.

The service

What our Google My Business service includes

Setup or claim

If you don't have a profile, we create it. If one already exists (sometimes Google creates it automatically, or a former employee opened it), we help you claim it and regain access.

Verification help

We explain the method Google assigns you and guide you through it so the process doesn't stall.

Full optimization

Primary and secondary categories, services or products, description, hours (including holidays), attributes, a link to WhatsApp or your website, and real photos.

Weekly posts

Every week we publish news, offers, events or recent work. You send us photos on WhatsApp when you have them; we write the copy.

Review replies

We reply to every Google review respectfully, the good ones and the tough ones, in your business's voice. Sensitive ones we review with you before posting.

Questions and answers

We prepare your business's frequently asked questions and monitor the ones users leave, so strangers' wrong answers don't stand.

Every month you also get a plain-language report with the searches you appeared in, views on Maps and search, calls, direction requests, website clicks and new reviews.

Verification

How Google Business Profile verification works

Verification is the step that confuses people most. Here's what Google's official help (support.google.com/business) says, in plain words.

MethodHow it worksWhat you should know
Phone or SMSGoogle sends a code to the business numberKeep that phone handy
EmailGoogle sends a code by emailCheck your spam folder too
Live or recorded videoYou show the business on cameraYou must be at the business during open hours, showing signage, equipment and proof that you manage it
PostcardA card with a code arrives at the addressUsually arrives within 14 days; the code expires after 30

Google chooses the method, not you and not us. Reviews can take up to 5 business days. And while you wait, don't edit the name, address or category: that can restart or complicate the process.

Google reviews

Real reviews, answered with respect

  • We give you a short link to ask happy customers for reviews on WhatsApp
  • We reply to every review, positive or negative, in your business's voice
  • We review sensitive replies with you before they go live
  • We help you report reviews that violate Google's policies (Google decides whether to remove them)
  • We never buy, invent or trade reviews: it's against Google's policies and puts your profile at risk

Example

A set-lunch restaurant in Chapinero gets a review saying the delivery arrived cold. A good reply thanks the customer, apologizes without excuses, explains what will improve and offers a way to make it right. Future customers read that reply as closely as the review.

Getting started

What we need from you to get started

We want this process to take work off your plate, not add to it. To start we only need the basics, and almost all of it can be sent on WhatsApp.

  • Your business's exact name, as it appears on your sign and with the Chamber of Commerce
  • Your location's address, or the areas you serve if you work at customers' locations
  • Your hours, including Saturdays, Sundays and holidays
  • Real photos of the premises, the team, your products or finished work
  • Access to the email used to create the profile, if one already exists
  • Availability for verification, if Google asks for a video at the business

Your data is handled carefully and in line with Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 on personal data protection. We never publish anything on your profile that you haven't approved in general terms, and you always keep ownership of the listing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are Google My Business and Google Business Profile the same thing?

Yes. Google renamed Google My Business, known in Colombia as Google Mi Negocio, to Google Business Profile in late 2021. It's the free listing that shows your business on Google Maps and in search, with your address, hours, photos, reviews and buttons to call or get directions.

How long does profile verification take?

It depends on the method Google assigns you. According to Google's official help, reviews can take up to 5 business days, and if they send you a postcard, the code usually arrives within 14 days and expires after 30. While you wait, avoid changing the name, address or category.

Can you verify the profile for me?

We help with the whole process, but Google decides on verification and some methods require you to be there. For example, video verification means recording at the business during open hours, showing your signage, equipment and proof that you manage it. We explain step by step what to record.

How often should I post on my Business Profile?

We recommend posting every week: news, offers, photos of real work or events. Posts show the business is active and give customers reasons to reach out. In our service we prepare them, and you just send us photos or ideas when you have them.

What should I do about a negative Google review?

Reply calmly, respectfully and promptly, without arguing or revealing customer details. Acknowledge the problem, offer a solution and a way to get in touch. If the review violates Google's policies it can be reported, but Google decides whether to remove it. We write the replies with you.

Does having a profile guarantee I'll show up first on Google Maps?

No. A complete, active profile greatly improves your chances, but Google ranks results by relevance, distance and prominence, and nobody controls that order. What we do guarantee is a well-built profile, maintained every week and measured every month.
