How it works

How to get customers for your business: the full journey

A great product isn't enough if no one can find it. Here's how we take a business from “no one knows us” to receiving customer messages, in four connected, measured steps.

Message us on WhatsAppGet your free quote
Quick answer

To get customers for your business you need four connected steps: get found on Google, Maps and AI; a website that impresses; an easy path to message you on WhatsApp; and AI agents that give you time back. Bogotá SEO starts with a free diagnosis.

The short answer

How to get customers for my business: get found, earn trust, get the message

To get customers for your business you need four connected things: people find you when they search for what you sell, your website shows why you're worth it, contacting you is effortless and someone replies fast. If one fails, customers drop off along the way. This page shows how we build each step.

Picture a Bogotá business with an excellent product. Customers who come in come back. People who try it recommend it. But the shop is half empty on weekdays, the phone doesn't ring the way it should and the owner wonders what they're doing wrong. The answer is almost always the same: the product is good, but people can't find it. When someone searches Google for “custom cakes near me” or asks their phone for a good repair shop, someone else shows up.

That's the story we want to change. Not with tricks, but with a clear journey that takes a person from the search to a conversation with you.

The map

A new customer's path, from search to WhatsApp

Step 0

Diagnosis: know where you stand before spending a peso

Before doing anything, we look. With the free SEO audit we review how your business shows up on Google, Google Maps and in AI answers; how fast your website is on a phone; what your reviews say; and whether a visitor has a clear path to message you.

That tells us which of the four steps is failing. Sometimes the problem is that nobody finds you. Other times they find you, but the website doesn't convince them. And often messages come in that nobody answers in time. Each case calls for something different, and there's no point paying for what you don't need.

Step 1 · Found

Get found on Google, on Maps and in artificial intelligence

If you don't show up when they search, to that customer you don't exist. This step is the foundation of attracting customers to your business without relying only on word of mouth.

Google Business Profile

Formerly “Google My Business.” We create or claim it, guide you through verification, choose the right categories, upload real photos and post regularly. See how we handle your Google Business Profile.

SEO (search engine optimization)

We improve your site's technical side, write content in Colombian Spanish that answers what people ask and build authority. More on SEO in Bogotá.

Local SEO

So people find you in your area: Chapinero, Suba, Kennedy, Usaquén or wherever you serve, and also in the Sabana. Reviews, consistent data and area pages. Learn about local SEO in Bogotá.

AI visibility

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. We organize your information so these tools understand it and can cite it. Read about AI search visibility.

Need visits while organic SEO gains momentum? Google, Facebook and Instagram ads (what Colombians call “pauta,” or ad spend) can speed up the start, measured peso by peso.

Step 2 · Impress

A website that shows what makes your business amazing

The person has arrived. Now you have a few seconds, almost always on a phone, to show why they should choose you.

  • Your real story: who's behind it, since when and why you do what you do.
  • Real photos: your shop, your team, your product. No stock photos seen on a thousand other sites.
  • Proof that exists: real Google reviews, certifications, years in business. We never make up testimonials.
  • A clear offer: what you sell, who it's for and what the next step is.
  • Phone first: fast, easy to read one-handed and with WhatsApp always in view.

That's our approach to web design in Bogotá. If you sell products, an online store lets you take orders and payments without watching over each one.

Step 3 · Convert

Make messaging you effortless, and reply right away

A visit doesn't pay the rent. A message, a call or a quote request can start a sale.

According to a Telefónica Movistar study presented at Andicom 2022, 8 out of 10 Colombians use WhatsApp to communicate. That's why we put it at the center:

  1. One-tap WhatsApp button

    Visible on every screen, with a pre-written opening message so the person doesn't have to think about what to say.

  2. An agent that answers instantly

    A WhatsApp chatbot or the website chat answers common questions, hours and reference prices, day or night, and hands off to a person whatever needs human attention.

  3. Short forms and simple quotes

    Few fields, only what's needed. Anyone who prefers to leave their details can do so without hassle.

  4. Follow-up

    Many sales are lost because nobody wrote back. We set up organized follow-up so no quote is left hanging.

Step 4 · Free time

AI agents that give you hours back, with a person in charge

Once business starts moving, a new problem shows up: time. AI agents for business take on repetitive tasks so you can focus on what only you can do.

  • Daily or weekly reports and summaries, ready without building them by hand.
  • Organized files: quotes, documents and customer data where they belong.
  • Friendly review requests after every sale.
  • Help with invoices and repetitive admin tasks.

All with rules you set and human quality control: we review what the agents do, and sensitive cases always go to a person. Learn more about business process automation.

We measure everything

A monthly report so you decide with data, not hunches

Every month we show you what we did and what happened: website visits, calls and WhatsApp clicks, direction requests on Maps, searches you show up for and how your reviews are doing. In plain language, no jargon.

We don't promise miracles or first place, because results vary with your competition and your starting point. We promise serious work, honesty and the best tools.

Example

What the journey could look like for a bakery in Galerías

Example

This is a hypothetical case to illustrate the process, not a real client. Imagine a pastry shop in Galerías with delicious custom-order cakes that lives on word of mouth and neighbors.

Step 0: the diagnosis shows its Google profile has the wrong category, few photos and a website that takes too long to load on a phone.

Step 1: the Google Business Profile is fixed, real photos of the cakes are uploaded and content is created for searches like “custom cakes in Teusaquillo.”

Step 2: the website tells the story of the family recipe, shows the catalog with its own photos and explains how orders and deliveries work.

Step 3: a WhatsApp button with the message “I'd like a quote for a cake” and an agent that answers flavors, sizes and delivery times at any hour.

Step 4: an agent organizes the week's orders, sends delivery reminders and asks for a review once the customer receives their cake.

Every business is different, and what works for a bakery isn't the same for a clinic or a repair shop. That's why we start with the diagnosis. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get more customers for my business in Bogotá?

First, get found: Google Business Profile, SEO and local SEO. Second, a website that shows why your business is worth choosing. Third, make contacting you effortless, with WhatsApp one tap away. And fourth, reply fast and follow up. When those four steps work together, more visits turn into conversations and sales.

Where do I start if my business barely shows up online?

Start with a diagnosis. We review, free of charge, how your business shows up on Google, Maps and AI tools, how fast your website loads on a phone and whether it converts. That tells us which step is missing, so you don't spend money on what you don't need.

How do I attract customers to my business without paying for ads all the time?

Organic SEO, a well-managed Google Business Profile and reviews bring you visits without paying per click. It takes longer than ads, but it builds up over time. Many businesses combine both: ads to get started and SEO to build for the long term.

How do I sell more online if I already have a website?

Check whether your site loads quickly on a phone, explains in seconds what you sell and why you're different, has real photos and keeps the WhatsApp button in view. Many websites get visits but don't convert. Fixing that is often the fastest win.

Do AI agents replace my team?

No. AI agents handle repetitive tasks like answering common questions, organizing files or putting together reports. Important decisions and sensitive cases stay with people, and we review the quality of the agents' work so your service stays excellent.

Do you guarantee results?

We don't promise miracles or first place on Google, because no honest company can. What we do is serious work with the best tools, and every month we show you what was done and what moved, so you can decide based on data.
