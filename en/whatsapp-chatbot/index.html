Short answer

What does a WhatsApp chatbot do for your business?

A WhatsApp chatbot with an AI agent replies within seconds to every customer who writes to you, at any hour, answers the usual questions and leaves appointments and quotes ready. When a case needs human judgment, it hands the conversation to your team with a summary. You stop living glued to your cell phone, and your customers feel well taken care of.

In Colombia, WhatsApp is where people talk to businesses. If a customer writes "do you deliver?" on a Saturday at 8 p.m. and nobody answers, they will probably message the next business on the list. A good AI agent on WhatsApp prevents that without you having to be available all day.

What it does

What your AI agent on WhatsApp can do

Answer the usual questions

Hours, address, payment methods, delivery, warranties: what your customers ask ten times a day, answered well and in your tone.

Show the catalog

It shares products or services with photos, descriptions and availability, and helps the customer choose.

Book appointments

It offers open time slots, confirms the appointment and sends a reminder the day before to reduce no-shows.

Collect quote details

It asks the questions your team needs (measurements, quantities, address, photos) and delivers a complete request.

Cover after-hours

At night, on holidays or weekends, the customer gets a reply and knows when a person will contact them.

Qualify and sort leads

It identifies who is ready to buy and passes those chats to your sales team first.

All of this builds on what our AI agents for business do: clear rules, a log of every action and a person reviewing what matters.

The handoff to a person

How the chatbot hands the conversation to your team

A good WhatsApp Business chatbot knows when to step aside. We define that moment with you from the start.

  • The customer asks to talk to a person: they are passed along right away.
  • The customer is upset or has a complaint: it goes to your team as a priority.
  • The question goes beyond what the agent knows: it does not make things up; it flags and transfers.
  • There is a large sale or a sensitive case: your team takes over.
  • Your team receives a summary so the customer does not have to repeat anything.

Respect for your customers

Opt-in, zero spam and data protection

WhatsApp Business works for companies when people trust you. That is why we work with clear rules:

  • Opt-in only: the agent writes first only to people who agreed to receive messages from you.
  • Useful messages: confirmations, reminders and replies, not a barrage of advertising.
  • Easy exit: customers can ask not to be contacted again, and that is respected.
  • Protected data: Law 1581 of 2012 governs personal data in Colombia; your business needs a data-treatment policy and your customers' consent.

How we set it up

From the first conversation to a working agent

  1. We listen to your chats

    We review with you what your customers ask, what times they write and which conversations end in a sale. That is the raw material for the agent.

  2. We write the replies and the rules

    We write the replies in your business's tone, define what it must never say (discounts, dates that do not depend on you, sensitive topics) and when it passes the chat to a person.

  3. We connect your tools

    Depending on what you need, we connect your calendar, your catalog or the sheet where you track quotes, so the agent does not just talk but gets the work done.

  4. Supervised pilot

    During the first weeks your team reviews the conversations and we correct whatever is needed before giving the agent more autonomy.

  5. Adjustments over time

    Prices change, new products arrive and different questions come up. We keep the agent up to date so it keeps answering well.

It is the same path we follow on all our projects; you can see it in full in how working with us works.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a WhatsApp chatbot?

It is an assistant that automatically answers the messages your customers send you on WhatsApp. It can answer common questions, show products, book appointments or collect details for a quote. When it is connected to artificial intelligence and to your tools, it becomes an AI agent that also carries out tasks and hands the chat to a person when needed.

Can the WhatsApp chatbot hand the conversation to a person?

Yes, and in the way we work it is mandatory. We define when the chat goes to your team: if the customer asks, if they are upset, if the question falls outside the rules or if an important sale is at stake. The person receives a summary of the conversation so the customer does not have to repeat everything.

Can I use a chatbot with the regular WhatsApp Business app?

The WhatsApp Business app includes basic tools such as greeting messages, away messages, quick replies and a catalog. For an AI agent connected to your calendar or inventory, the official WhatsApp platform for businesses is used. During the assessment we tell you which option suits you based on your message volume.

Can a WhatsApp chatbot be used to send mass advertising?

We do not recommend it and we do not work that way. Messages only go to people who gave their permission, with useful content and an option to stop receiving them. In addition, Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 requires consent to handle personal data. Spam damages your number's reputation and your customers' trust.

Can my business answer WhatsApp at night and on weekends?

Yes. The agent replies at any hour, resolves frequent questions, takes the customer's details and leaves the appointment or quote request ready. Anything that needs a person is queued for your team to handle on the next business day, and the customer knows exactly when they will hear back.

How much does a WhatsApp Business chatbot cost?

It depends on how many tasks it handles, whether it connects to your calendar, catalog or inventory, your message volume and whether you use the app or the official platform. That is why we prefer to understand your case first. Write to us and we will prepare a free quote with a clear scope.
