Quick answer

We manage Google, Facebook and Instagram ads for businesses in Bogotá and Colombia, with landing pages that convert, clear tracking and honest testing. We don't guarantee sales, but we measure every step so each peso works harder.

The short answer

Is it worth advertising on Google and social media?

Advertising on Google, Facebook or Instagram is worth it when it's well targeted, leads to a page that converts and is truly measured. Without that, it's money spent with no idea what worked. We build the campaigns, the landing page and the tracking as one system, and show you results in plain language.

In Colombia, ad spend is called "pauta": paying for your ad to appear in front of the right people. The problem usually isn't the ad; it's what happens after the click. If people land on a slow, confusing page with no WhatsApp button, that click is wasted. That's why our ads are always connected to a website that converts.

Where we advertise

Google Ads, Meta and remarketing, each with its own role

Google ads

Your ad appears when someone searches for what you offer, for example "plumber in Cedritos." It reaches people who already have the need. It includes search and, when it applies, ads on Google Maps.

Facebook and Instagram ads

Ads for people who fit your ideal customer by area, interests and behavior. Ideal for showing products, launches and offers with photos and reels.

Remarketing

We remind people who visited your site but didn't message you about your offer. In moderation and in line with privacy policies.

Budget

How much should you spend on ads? How to think about it without guessing

We won't give you a magic number. What we can do is explain what drives the budget so you decide with real information.

FactorWhy it matters
Cost per click in your industrySome services are highly competitive on Google and each click costs more.
Size of the areaA neighborhood, a district (localidad), all of Bogotá or the whole country require different amounts.
Data to learnPlatforms need enough clicks and conversions to optimize campaigns.
Value of a customerIf a new customer is worth a lot to your business, it makes sense to invest more to win one.
SeasonDates like Amor y Amistad (Colombia's September friendship day) or December change competition and costs.

Let's be honest: the first stage of any campaign is a test. We test messages, audiences and offers, measure, and keep what works. Some tests don't turn out as expected, and that's valuable information too. What matters is that your test budget is one you can comfortably sustain.

Transparency

How we look after every peso of your ad spend

Accounts in your business's name

We recommend that ad and analytics accounts be in your business's name. That way the data and history belong to you.

Keywords that don't help you

On Google we exclude searches unrelated to what you sell, so you don't pay for clicks from people looking for something else.

Area and schedule

If you only serve certain districts of Bogotá or the Sabana, or during certain hours, the ads adjust to that.

Honest ads

No exaggerated promises in the ads. Besides being the right thing to do, platforms often reject that kind of message.

When advertising on Instagram, content matters as much as targeting. A reel shot in your own business, with your people and your real products, usually connects better than a stock photo. That's why we work hand in hand with the social media team so your ads look like you.

Ads + everything else

Ads work better when they don't work alone

Ads bring visits while ranking on Google grows over time; the two complement each other. If your ads lead to WhatsApp, a WhatsApp chatbot or an AI agent can give the first reply at any hour, always with human oversight. And the content that works in ads often comes from social media management.

Example

A dental office in Chapinero could run Google ads for "teeth whitening Chapinero," send the click to a page only about that treatment with a WhatsApp button, and use Instagram remarketing to remind people who visited the page. Each step is measured to see which one brings booked appointments.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much money do I need to advertise on Google or Facebook?

There's no single number. Your budget depends on how much each click costs in your industry, how large an area you want to cover and how much data the campaign needs to learn. The sensible approach is to start with a test budget you can sustain, measure, and then decide whether to scale.

Is it better to advertise on Google or on Facebook?

It depends on your business. Google works very well when people are already searching for what you sell. Facebook and Instagram help you show your product to people who aren't searching yet but fit your ideal customer. Many businesses use both, each with a different role.

Will paid ads guarantee me sales?

No. Nobody can guarantee sales from advertising. Ads bring you visits and opportunities; whether they turn into customers also depends on your offer, your website, your WhatsApp service and your price. What we do is measure every step and improve whatever isn't working.

What is remarketing?

It means showing ads to people who already visited your website or interacted with your business but didn't contact you. It reminds them of your offer when they're ready to decide. It should be used in moderation and in line with privacy and personal data policies.

Why do ads need a landing page?

Because sending an ad to your home page or a general profile forces people to search again for what they saw. A landing page talks about exactly what the ad promised and has one next step, such as messaging on WhatsApp. That usually makes better use of every peso you spend.

How often will I see results from my ads?

We share a plain-language report showing what was spent, how many people saw the ads, how many clicked and how many messaged, called or bought when that can be measured. We review campaigns continuously and tell you what we changed and why.
