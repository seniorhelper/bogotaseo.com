Quick answer

Short answer

What is an SEO audit and what does ours include?

An SEO audit is a review of how Google and your customers see your website: whether Google can read it, how fast it loads on a phone, how your business appears on Maps and what's keeping you from getting more customers. Ours is free, with no obligation, and also covers your Google Business Profile, your reviews and your visibility in AI tools. You get a clear report with what's working, what's broken and where to start.

Many business owners in Bogotá feel their website "doesn't work," but they don't know why. Sometimes it's a technical problem nobody has noticed; sometimes the site is fine but there's no visible WhatsApp button. The audit puts it all in black and white.

What we check

What our SEO audit in Bogotá reviews

Indexing

Whether Google finds and stores your pages, or whether pages are blocked, duplicated or broken.

Mobile speed

How long it takes to load on a phone with mobile data, which is how most people visit.

Titles and descriptions

Whether each page clearly says what it offers and where, using the words people search for.

Business Profile

Verification, category, hours, photos, posts and consistent details.

Reviews

How many you have, how recent they are and whether they're being answered.

Structured data

Whether your site has the code that tells Google what your business is and where it serves.

AI visibility

How some AI tools describe your business, and whether they get it right.

Conversion

Whether the WhatsApp button is visible, the forms work and it's easy to call.

What you get

What you receive with the free SEO audit

  • A report in plain language, without unnecessary jargon (in Spanish or English)
  • A traffic-light rating for each area: what's fine, what can improve and what's urgent
  • The three to five priorities that could have the biggest impact in your case
  • An idea of where your site ranks on Google today for a few key searches
  • The option to go over it with us on WhatsApp or a video call

Example

A clothing store in Galerías has a nice-looking website, but on a phone the WhatsApp button is hidden at the bottom and the photos are so heavy the page is slow to open. Neither problem is noticeable from the office computer. Fixing that may be more urgent than writing new content.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the SEO audit really free?

Yes. We don't charge for the audit, and you don't have to sign anything or commit to hiring us. We do it because it's the best way to show you how we work. If you later want help with the improvements, we send a separate quote and you decide in your own time.

What do I need to request the audit?

Just your business name, your website address if you have one, your city or neighborhood and which services or products you want to sell more of. You can send it on WhatsApp or through the contact form. If you don't have a website, we still review your Google Business Profile and social media.

How long does it take to get the audit?

It depends on how many requests we have at the time and the size of your website. We confirm the estimated time as soon as we get your details on WhatsApp, and we let you know when the report is ready to go over with you.

How can I tell where my website ranks on Google?

The most reliable way is Google Search Console, which shows the searches your site appears in, its clicks and its average position. If you don't have it set up, the audit explains how to do it, and we manually check a few key searches for your business.

Are you going to call me to sell me something?

We won't pressure you. We send you the report, offer to walk you through it and, if you want, explain how we could help. If you'd rather make the improvements yourself or with someone else, the report is yours and it's just as useful.

Does the audit include visibility in ChatGPT?

Yes. We check how some AI tools describe your business when asked about your category and area, and whether the information they give is correct. We explain what could be improved, without promising results nobody controls.
