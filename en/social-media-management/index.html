Quick answer

We manage social media for businesses in Bogotá with content plans, reels, comment and message handling, and a direct connection to your WhatsApp and website, so followers turn into conversations with your business.

The short answer

What does good social media management do for your business?

Good social media management helps people get to know your business, trust it and message you. It's not about posting for the sake of posting: it's about consistently showing what's real about your business and leading every interested follower to WhatsApp or your website. That's the work we do in Bogotá.

Many business owners know they should be on social media, but between serving customers, running the shop and keeping the books, Instagram gets left for "when there's time." The result is a profile whose last post is months old, which costs trust with anyone who comes looking.

What we do

A community manager in Bogotá with a method, not improvisation

Content plan

A monthly calendar with topics that answer what your customers ask, key dates and what's happening in your business. You approve it before anything is posted.

Reels and short video

Ideas, simple scripts and editing. We often shoot with your own phone: authentic connects better than perfect.

Community and replies

We handle comments and messages during agreed hours, with replies you approve. Anything that needs your judgment, we pass to you.

Clear report

Every month we tell you which posts worked, how many people messaged and what we'll change.

Social + WhatsApp + web

How social media connects to WhatsApp and your website

Social media is the shop window. WhatsApp is the counter where the conversation happens. Your website is the whole store, with all the information and trust. When all three work together, a curious follower becomes a customer.

  • WhatsApp link in your bio and in posts with an offer.
  • Links to specific pages on your website, not just the home page.
  • Saved quick replies for the usual questions.
  • An optional WhatsApp chatbot to reply after hours.

The difference

Posting for the sake of it vs. social media with a strategy

Without a strategy

  • Generic images from the internet
  • Posts whenever there's time
  • Messages unanswered for days
  • Measured in likes
  • Nobody knows how to contact you

With a strategy

  • Real photos and videos of your business
  • A consistent, approved calendar
  • Timely replies in your tone
  • Measured in conversations and customers
  • WhatsApp always one tap away

Example

A bakery in Teusaquillo could post a short reel every time the morning bread comes out, show special birthday orders and pin a post with the WhatsApp link for custom orders. On rainy days, which in Bogotá arrive when you least expect them, a story about "hot chocolate and almojábana" (a Colombian cheese bread) can invite delivery orders.

Content ideas

What to post so people message you

The best content for a business isn't the most elaborate, but the one that shows something real and useful. These formats tend to work well for businesses in Bogotá:

Behind the scenes

How the product is made, how the work gets done, who's behind it. It builds closeness.

Common questions

The questions you get on WhatsApp, answered in a short video. It saves time and builds trust.

Before and after

For services like remodeling, beauty or cleaning, with real photos and the customer's permission.

News and seasons

New products, special dates, holiday hours and what's happening in your neighborhood.

Your team

The people who serve your customers. People buy from people.

Real reviews

Genuine opinions from your customers, shared with their permission. Never invented.

We also keep things consistent: the same tone, colors and information on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or whichever network makes sense for your business. You don't need to be everywhere; it's better to do two well than five badly. If your customers are other companies, LinkedIn may matter more than TikTok; if you sell food or beauty services in your neighborhood, Instagram and WhatsApp are usually the main players.

With a plan like this, the community manager doesn't improvise every day: they know what to post, why, and where to lead anyone who's interested.

Social media advertising

When it makes sense to add Facebook and Instagram ads

Posting helps with people who already follow you. To reach new people consistently, Facebook and Instagram ads amplify the posts that have already proven they work. According to IAB Colombia, cited by Impacto TIC, social media holds close to 30% of the digital advertising market in Colombia: it's where many brands are investing.

  1. First, solid content

  2. Then, find what connects

  3. After that, focused ads

    We invest in those posts, targeted to your area and ideal customer, with tracking.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does social media management include?

It includes a monthly content plan, creating or editing posts and reels, scheduling, handling comments and messages as agreed, and a simple report on what worked. We also connect your social profiles to your website and your WhatsApp so followers can become customers.

How often should I post on Instagram or Facebook?

Rather than a fixed number, consistency and quality matter. It's better to post less often with useful, real content than to fill your profile with generic images. We set a frequency your business can sustain and adjust it based on how your audience responds.

Do you answer the messages and comments on my social media?

We can, within agreed hours and with replies you approve for common questions. When a message needs your judgment, like a special price or a complaint, we pass it to you. If you want replies at any hour, a WhatsApp chatbot can give the first response and hand the conversation to a person.

Does social media actually help sell?

It helps people get to know you, trust you and message you. The sale usually closes on WhatsApp, on your website or at your location. That's why we don't just measure followers or likes, but how many people move from social media to a conversation with your business, which is what really matters.

Do I need to run social ads or is posting enough?

Posting helps with people who already follow you. To reach new people consistently, a moderate Facebook and Instagram ad budget almost always helps. We recommend starting with solid content and adding ads once we know which posts connect best with your audience.
