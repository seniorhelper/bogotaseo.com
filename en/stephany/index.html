A picture is worth a thousand words

This photo comes from Stephany's family album. It's a Bogotá patio like so many others: tiled floor, laundry on the line, a wall that has seen plenty of downpours. And there she is, as a little girl.

Stephany was born in Colombia and spent her childhood in Bogotá. She moved to the United States in her mid teens. She changed countries, but some things don't move: pride in her roots, love for the Colombian people, and a family that still lives there and is on her mind every day.

The mission

Give the businesses of her city the same tools as the biggest companies in the world

Stephany knows the drive of Colombian entrepreneurs firsthand: people who get up early, serve customers with warmth, and make excellent products and services. She also knows those businesses often don't have access to the technology big companies use.

That's why her goal is to bring the world's most powerful advertising tools to Bogotá and all of Colombia: rankings on Google and Google Maps, websites that sell, well-measured ad spend, and artificial intelligence agents that give business owners their time back.
