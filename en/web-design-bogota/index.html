The short answer

What makes a good website for a business in Bogotá?

A good website loads fast on a phone, explains in seconds what your business does and why it is different, and leads the visitor to message you on WhatsApp, call or request a quote. That is our job in web design in Bogotá: making your business look the way it really is and giving every visit a clear path to becoming a customer.

Many businesses in Bogotá have an excellent product or service, but online they look like everyone else. Some have an old website that doesn't work on phones; some depend only on Instagram; some have nothing at all. People search, don't find what they need, and go with the first business that earns their trust.

Our approach follows four steps: first, people find you on Google; then your website shows what makes your business special; then it converts the visit into a conversation; and finally, technology gives you time back. Your website is at the center of all of it. You can see the full journey in how it works.

What's included

Websites for companies and businesses, built to sell

We don't build websites to look pretty in a presentation. We build them to work on the phone of a customer riding TransMilenio, with a weak signal and little time.

Mobile first

We design for the small screen first and then adapt to computers. Big buttons, readable text and nothing you have to pinch to zoom.

Truly fast

Clean code, optimized images and no heavy decoration. A slow site loses visitors before they see what you offer.

WhatsApp button

Always visible, with an opening message that tells you which page they came from. You know what service the customer wants from the first hello.

SEO from day one

Structure, titles, descriptions, structured data and content built around what people actually search for. Rankings start during the build.

Conversion paths

Every section leads to an action: message, call, book an appointment or request a quote. Visitors never wonder what comes next.

Ley 1581 and trust

If your site collects names, emails or phone numbers, we include the data-treatment policy and consent required by Colombia's Ley 1581 of 2012 (habeas data).

Show, don't invent

Your website should show what makes your business great

What convinces a customer isn't nice phrases like "quality and commitment." What convinces is seeing the person, the place, the finished work and real proof.

That's why, before designing, we sit down with you to find what makes your business special: how it started, what you do differently, why your current customers come back. Then we show it with what you actually have:

  • Your story, told in a few lines and in your own voice, not a template.
  • Real photos of your team, your location, your products or your work. If you don't have good photos yet, we tell you what to shoot and how.
  • Proof that exists: your real Google reviews, certifications, years in business, finished work.
  • Answers to the questions your customers ask you every day on WhatsApp.
  • Clear details: where you are, which areas you serve, hours, whether you deliver.

We never use invented testimonials, logos of clients that aren't yours or numbers that can't be verified. An honest website takes a little longer to put together, but it builds a kind of trust no trick can replace.

Price

How much does a website cost in Colombia?

The honest answer: it depends on what your business needs. Be wary of a flat price that asks nothing about your business. Here's what really drives the cost:

Size and number of pages

A one-section site is not the same as one with pages for each service, neighborhood or city.

Content and photos

Whether the text already exists or has to be written, and whether photos need organizing or producing.

Special features

Online store, payments, bookings, quote calculators, client areas or multi-step forms.

Integrations and AI

WhatsApp, a WhatsApp chatbot, analytics tools or AI agents that answer first inquiries.

SEO level

The foundations are included; a content strategy to compete for difficult searches is a bigger job.

Payment model

Per-project payment or the subscribe-to-own program.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Colombia?

It depends on the number of pages, whether you need an online store or bookings, how much content and photography has to be prepared, integrations such as WhatsApp or a chatbot, and the SEO work involved. That is why we don't publish a price list. Tell us what your business needs and we will send you a clear, free, no-obligation quote.

How long does it take to build a website for a business?

A simple website can be ready in a few weeks if the content and photos are available. A custom project, an online store or a site with many sections takes longer. The thing that slows projects down most is gathering text, photos and details, so we help you organize them from the very first day.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build every site on solid SEO foundations: speed, structure, titles, structured data and useful content. That gives your site the opportunity to appear. We don't promise rankings or first place, because nobody can guarantee them. If you want to work on rankings over time, we do that through a separate SEO plan.

Can I have a WhatsApp button on my website?

Yes, and we recommend it. In Colombia most people would rather send a WhatsApp message than fill out a form. We place the button where it is always visible on phones and computers, with an opening message that tells you which page they came from, and we can connect it to a chatbot that answers the first questions.

Do you build websites on WordPress or Shopify?

Yes. We build everything from simple websites to fully custom development, including WordPress and Shopify. We choose the tool based on what your business needs, who will update the site and how you plan to grow. We explain the pros of each option in plain language before you decide.

What is the subscribe-to-own program?

It is a way to get a custom website by paying a monthly subscription instead of one large upfront payment, with the option to buy the site later. It works well for businesses that want a serious website without tying up their cash. We explain how it would work for you in your quote.
