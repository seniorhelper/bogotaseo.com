A team that combines a love for Colombia with world-class marketing and artificial intelligence technology, so Bogotá's good businesses get found.

Quick answer

Bogotá SEO is a team that helps businesses in Bogotá and Colombia grow online. Stephany serves you in Spanish, and the technology comes from Eye To Ad Media, headquartered in Denver, Colorado, since 2012, with 60 Google reviews and an A+ BBB accreditation.

In a few words

We help Colombia's good businesses get found

Bogotá SEO helps small businesses and entrepreneurs in Bogotá and across Colombia show up on Google, Maps and artificial intelligence, have a website that shows what makes them special and turn visits into customers on WhatsApp. Stephany serves you in Spanish, backed by a team with more than a decade of building marketing that works.

We are a team that brings two worlds together. On one side, someone who knows Bogotá from the inside: how people shop in the neighborhood, how they order deliveries and why a Bogotá customer would rather send a WhatsApp than call. On the other, a technical team that has spent years building websites, SEO and marketing systems for businesses in different countries and industries.

We believe something simple: Bogotá is full of excellent businesses nobody knows about. The neighborhood bakery, the clinic in Chapinero, the repair shop in Restrepo, the service firm in Usaquén. They don't lack quality; they lack being found. Our job is to close that gap with honesty and the best tools.

Our values

What we don't compromise on

Honesty without promises

No honest company can guarantee first place on Google or a number of sales. We tell you what we'll do, how we measure it and how strong your competition is. We don't promise miracles; we promise serious work, honesty and the best tools.

We test at home first

The tactics we use with clients we first test on our own businesses. If something doesn't work for us, we don't sell it to you.

People in charge of AI

Artificial intelligence is a powerful tool, but it doesn't decide on its own. We review what the agents do, and important cases always go to a person.

Respect for data

We protect your information and your customers' in line with Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012. Read our data-treatment policy.

Colombian pride

We write in Colombian Spanish, understand how people buy and sell here, and love watching this country's entrepreneurs grow.

Clarity in everything

Monthly reports in plain language, no jargon. You always know what was done and why.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is behind Bogotá SEO?

Stephany, a Bogotá native at heart, leads the client relationship in Spanish. The technology and technical team come from Eye To Ad Media, headquartered in Denver, Colorado, which has built websites, SEO and marketing systems since 2012 and works with businesses all over the world.

Does Bogotá SEO have good reviews?

The Eye To Ad Media team has 60 Google reviews with a 5.0 average and an A+ accreditation from the BBB, the US organization that rates business trustworthiness. They are real reviews from real clients; we don't publish made-up testimonials on this page.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small and midsize businesses, startups and companies in Bogotá, the Sabana and all of Colombia that have a good product or service and want more people to find them: restaurants, clinics, repair shops, stores, service firms and many more. We also work with businesses in other countries.

How do you protect my customers' data?

We follow Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 on personal data protection. We only use data for what you authorize, store it with security measures and help make sure your own website has a clear data-treatment policy and consent.

Do you use artificial intelligence for everything?

We use AI where it truly saves time, but people stay in charge. We review what the agents do, sensitive cases go to a human, and important decisions are made by you with our team.
