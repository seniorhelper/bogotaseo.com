Quick answer

We are a digital marketing agency in Bogotá that brings SEO, Google Business Profile, websites, ads, social media and AI agents together in one plan, so your business gets found, gets chosen and gets messages. We don't promise miracles; we promise serious work.

The short answer

What does our digital marketing agency in Bogotá do?

We bring every piece of digital marketing together in one plan: your business shows up on Google, Maps and AI search; your website shows what makes you special; every visit ends in a WhatsApp chat, a call or a form; and technology gives you time back. All of it measured and explained in plain language.

Bogotá is full of businesses with excellent products and services that almost nobody finds. It isn't a lack of quality: online they look like everyone else, or they don't show up at all. Hiring one thing here and another there rarely works, because the pieces don't talk to each other. Our job is to make them talk.

How we work

How we work, with no fine print

  • Diagnosis first. We review where your business stands today before recommending anything.
  • Clear priorities. We don't sell you everything at once; we start with what can have the most impact in your case.
  • Everything is measured. Visits, WhatsApp messages, calls, forms. We show you the data in plain language.
  • Nothing fake. No bought reviews, no invented testimonials, no tricks that put your business at risk.
  • AI with human oversight. We use artificial intelligence to work faster, but a person checks the quality.
  • Personal data done right. Forms and databases with a data-treatment policy under Colombia's Ley 1581 of 2012.

We don't promise miracles; we promise serious work, honesty and the best tools. Nobody can guarantee first place on Google or a number of sales, and you should be wary of anyone who does.

What to expect in the first months

The first month is usually diagnosis and foundations: Business Profile, WhatsApp, tracking and the most urgent website fixes. Then come content, SEO and, if it makes sense, ads. Each month we tell you what was done, what was measured and what's next. Some things show up quickly; others, like SEO, take time. We tell you that from the start.

We also don't ask you to understand algorithms or technical terms. If something can be explained more simply, we explain it more simply. SEO means ranking on search engines; ad spend is paid advertising; a conversion is when a visit becomes a message, a call or a purchase. That's enough to make good decisions together.

Although our focus is Bogotá, Chía, Cota, Funza and the rest of the Sabana, we work with businesses all over Colombia, and the team behind Bogotá SEO works with businesses all over the world.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a digital marketing agency do?

It helps your business get found online, look trustworthy and turn visits into customers. That includes ranking on Google, your Google Maps profile, your website, paid ads, social media and, increasingly, automation with artificial intelligence. What matters is that it all works together and is measured.

Where do I start if my business has nothing online?

We almost always start with your Google Business Profile and a well-configured WhatsApp, because they're quick and low cost. Then comes the website, content and ads. We give you a free diagnosis to tell you what makes sense first in your case.

Do you only work with companies in Bogotá?

Our focus is Bogotá and the Sabana, but we work with businesses all over Colombia. The technology and team behind Bogotá SEO are headquartered in Denver, Colorado, and work with businesses all over the world. Spanish-language service is led by Stephany.

Do you guarantee results?

No. No serious agency can guarantee Google rankings or sales, because they depend on many factors outside anyone's control. What we do guarantee is serious work, transparency about what we do and clear measurement so you can make decisions based on data.

Do you give clients free marketing?

Sometimes, as a thank-you, we give loyal clients and people who refer good clients bonus marketing at no charge, sometimes worth thousands of dollars. It's a thank-you, not a promise or a sign-up incentive: don't sign up expecting free marketing.

How do I get in touch with you?
