Business process automation: fewer repetitive tasks, more time for your business

We automate administrative and marketing tasks with virtual assistants and AI agents, always with a person reviewing what matters.

Quick answer

Business process automation has software handle repetitive tasks like files, reports, invoices and email, following rules you approve and with a person reviewing what matters. Start with the task that takes up most of your time.

Short answer

What is process automation and what is it good for?

Business process automation means having software handle your business's repetitive tasks: organizing files, creating reports, reminding customers about invoices, answering frequent emails or posting on social media. You set the rules, the system does the work and a person reviews what matters. The result is time back to serve customers, grow or simply live.

Many entrepreneurs in Bogotá finish their workday and start a second shift: the office one. Moving invoices into a folder, checking who owes what, answering the same emails, updating inventory. None of that is what makes your business special, but all of it eats up hours. We can automate almost any of those tasks, always with human quality control.

The office on autopilot

Automating administrative tasks

Files and documents

Invoices, contracts, supplier tax registrations (RUT) and receipts are renamed and saved automatically in the right folder.

Reports

The week's sales, expenses and results pulled together into a draft report you can review in minutes.

Invoices and collections

Courteous payment reminders and a list of who needs a call from you. Electronic invoicing with the DIAN (Colombia's tax authority) is still handled by your accountant or your invoicing system.

Simple inventory

Alerts when a product drops below the minimum and a summary of what moves the most.

Frequent emails

Draft replies to the usual questions, ready for you to approve or send.

Form data

What comes in through your website goes straight into your spreadsheet or customer list, no copying and pasting.

Marketing that never forgets

Marketing automation

Marketing automation makes the good things happen every time, not just when someone remembers:

The virtual assistant for business

When several of these tasks come together in a single digital helper, we call it a virtual assistant for business: an AI agent that reads, organizes, prepares drafts and reminds you of pending items. See how we design them on our AI agents for business page.

What to automate first

Scoring checklist: which task should you automate first?

Take one task from your week and give it one point for each statement that is true. Repeat with other tasks and compare.

  • It repeats at least several times a week.
  • It takes more than an hour a week from you or your team.
  • It follows clear rules you could explain to a new hire in ten minutes.
  • The information it needs is already digital (email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, folders).
  • A mistake is easy to fix if a person reviews it in time.
  • When it is forgotten or delayed, you lose money or a customer gets upset.
ScoreWhat it means
5 to 6An excellent candidate to start with.
3 to 4A good option, perhaps after tidying up the data a bit.
0 to 2Better to leave it for later or keep it in a person's hands.

Examples in Bogotá

What it could look like for businesses in the city

These cases are hypothetical and only illustrate how automation is applied.

Example

A bakery in Teusaquillo takes delivery orders on WhatsApp and writes down in a notebook what is running out. With automation, orders are logged in a spreadsheet, the system alerts when flour or packaging drops below the minimum, and every Monday a report arrives with the best sellers. The owner reviews the report over her morning coffee and plans the week's baking.

Example

A medical office in Chapinero loses appointments because patients forget. An agent books appointments on WhatsApp, sends the reminder the day before and alerts the front desk if someone cancels, so the slot can be offered to someone else. The agent never answers medical questions: those always go to the professional.

Example

An auto repair shop in Fontibón sends quotes that nobody follows up on. Automation reminds the customer a few days later, saves photos and documents for each car in its own folder and gives the owner a list of pending quotes every Friday.

Step by step

How we automate your processes

  1. We map the task

    We sit down with you or whoever does the task today and note every step: where the information comes from, what decisions are made and where it ends.

  2. We organize the data

    If prices, customers or inventory live in three different places, we first bring them into one. Without organized data, automation repeats the mistakes.

  3. We build and test

    We set up the automation with drafts for approval where it matters and test it with real cases under your team's review.

  4. We measure the time saved

    We compare how long the task took before and how long it takes now, and use that to decide together which one comes next.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is business process automation?

It means having software handle tasks someone currently does by hand over and over, like saving invoices, copying data, sending reminders or putting together reports. The process follows rules you approve, keeps a record of what it does and alerts a person when something is out of the ordinary.

Which administrative tasks can a small business automate?

The most common are organizing files and receipts, creating weekly reports, reminding customers about invoices due, flagging when a product is running low, answering frequent emails and moving data from a form into a spreadsheet. Almost any repetitive task with clear rules is a candidate.

What is a virtual assistant for business?

It is an artificial intelligence agent that works as digital administrative support. It can read email, organize documents, prepare draft reports or replies and remind you of pending items. Unlike a person, it works at any hour, but it still needs clear rules and human review of what matters.

Where should I start automating my business?

Start with the task that repeats the most, takes the most time and has clear rules. Use our scoring checklist to compare your tasks. Ideally, begin with one or two automations, measure how much time they give back and then grow calmly from there.

Does marketing automation replace my community manager?

No. Marketing automation takes care of the repetitive parts, like scheduling posts, sending reminders to customers who gave permission or asking for reviews. Strategy, creativity and your brand's voice still need people. Ideally, automation frees up your team's time so they can think better.

How much does it cost to automate processes in my company?

It depends on how many tasks you want to automate, how many tools need to be connected, the volume of work and the level of human review. Every business is different, so we prefer to understand your case before giving a figure. Write to us and we will send you a free quote.
