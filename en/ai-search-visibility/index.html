AI search visibility: help AI understand and mention your business

Short answer

What is AI search visibility?

AI search visibility (also called GEO or AIO) is the work of helping tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answers understand your business well and mention it when someone asks about what you offer. It's done with clear, consistent information, structured data, direct answers on your website and mentions on trusted sites. And it has to be said honestly: nobody controls what an AI answers.

The way people search is changing. Many no longer type "restaurant Usaquén"; they ask an assistant: "where can I get a good, affordable lunch near Usaquén park?" The AI puts together an answer and sometimes names specific businesses. If the information about your business is confusing or thin, you're unlikely to be on that list.

That doesn't mean Google has stopped mattering. It means there are now more doors through which a customer can reach your business, and they all open with the same key: clear, real, trustworthy information. A neighborhood business in Kennedy or a company with several locations in the Sabana area can prepare just as well.

What we do

How we work to help you show up in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity

Entity clarity

Making it equally clear everywhere who you are: your name, what you do, where you serve, since when and how to reach you. Your website, Business Profile, social media and directories should all tell the same story.

Structured data

Schema.org code on your website (local business, services, FAQs) that tells machines, without ambiguity, what everything is.

Direct answers

Pages that open by answering the question in two or three sentences, like this one. It's the easiest format to understand and cite, for people and for AI.

Mentions on trusted sites

Presence in recognized Colombian directories, media and trade associations, plus real Google reviews. These are the sources AI often checks to confirm a business exists and is legitimate.

We also prepare an llms.txt file at the root of your website: an organized summary of your business and main pages, designed for language models. It's a recent proposal and not an official standard, so we treat it as a helpful extra, not a magic formula.

Honesty

What can and can't be done with AI

What nobody can do

  • Guarantee that ChatGPT or Gemini will recommend you
  • Control the exact answer an AI gives
  • Pay an AI to mention you in its regular answers
  • Ensure the answer is the same for everyone

What we do

  • Improve the quality and consistency of your information
  • Fix old or wrong details about your business
  • Create content that answers real questions
  • Periodically check how these tools describe your business

Example

A dental office in Cedritos moved two years ago, but several directories still show the old address. When someone asks an AI assistant, it might give the wrong address. Unifying the details everywhere is one of the first steps of the work.

The tools

Where your business can appear: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google

Each tool works differently, but they all need the same thing from you: clear, consistent, easy-to-verify information.

ChatGPT

It can answer from what it learned in training or search the web in the moment. A well-written, well-linked website improves the chances it finds you.

Gemini

Google's assistant. It draws on the Google ecosystem, so a complete Business Profile and a well-ranked website are an important foundation.

Perplexity

It searches the web and shows the sources it uses in each answer. Content with direct answers and clear facts is easier to cite.

Google AI Overviews

The AI-generated summaries that appear above some Google results, with links to the pages they use as sources.

What doesn't change from one tool to the next is the essential part: your business has to be easy to understand. That's why this work isn't a trend separate from SEO; it's SEO's natural evolution. The same foundations that help Google trust you also help AI.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get ChatGPT to recommend my business?

Nobody can force ChatGPT to recommend you. What you can do is give AI clear, consistent and trustworthy information about your business: a website that answers questions directly, structured data, a complete Google Business Profile and mentions on recognized sites. That increases the chances it will take you into account.

What are GEO and AIO?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization and AIO for AI Optimization. Both names describe the work of making your information easy for tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI-generated answers to understand and cite.

Does AI visibility replace SEO?

No, it complements it. Many AI tools consult search results and web pages to answer, so a well-ranked, well-written website is the foundation. AI-focused work adds clarity about who you are, direct answers and trust signals.

Can you guarantee I'll show up in ChatGPT?

No, and be wary of anyone who guarantees it. AI answers change depending on the question, the person, the moment and the model. What we do is improve the quality and consistency of information about your business and periodically check how these tools describe it.

What is an llms.txt file?

It's a text file placed at the root of your website with an organized summary of who you are and your most important pages, designed to be easy for language models to read. It's a recent proposal, not an official standard, so we use it as a helpful extra.

Does this work for a small business in Bogotá?

Yes. More and more people ask AI things like where to get a computer repaired in Suba or which bakery to try in Usaquén. A small business with clear information and real reviews can have the same foundations as a big one.
